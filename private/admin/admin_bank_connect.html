Step 1 — Institution Identity
Register your bank's legal name and BIC so other institutions can discover and verify you on the network.
Your BIC (Bank Identifier Code) is your institution's globally unique address on the SWIFT network. Once registered, other banks use this to route payments to you. Choose it carefully — it cannot be changed without notifying all connected counterparties.

Secure Storage Steps
1
Save to a Secret Manager Store the downloaded PEM in AWS KMS, HashiCorp Vault, Azure Key Vault, or your HSM. Never store it in version control.
2
Set Restrictive File Permissions If storing on disk temporarily: chmod 600 finanza_*.pem. Only the service account should have read access.
3
Share Your Public Key with Partner Banks Copy your public key (shown above) and send it to each institution you wish to connect with. They use it to verify signed payloads from Finanza.
4
Rotate Keys Annually Regenerate your key pair at least once per year or immediately if a breach is suspected. Notify all connected counterparties with the new public key.
